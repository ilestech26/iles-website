About ILES

Integrated engineering, built on compliance.

Integrity Lifting & Engineering Solutions Ltd (ILES) provides integrated engineering, inspection, testing, training, and energy services to industrial clients across West Africa.

Our business portfolio is structured to support safe operations, regulatory compliance, and asset reliability across multiple sectors — from oil & gas to mining.

“Our mission is to deliver cost-effective, high-quality, and transparent compliance solutions across diverse markets.”
